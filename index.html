▶ Screening Room

A private YouTube player for one household's touchscreen.

Screening Room is a personal, non-commercial app that runs on its owner's own computer. It searches YouTube and plays videos on a landscape touchscreen. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Optional YouTube sign-in

The owner can connect their own YouTube account with Google's sign-in for TVs and limited-input devices. Read-only access lets the app list the owner's subscriptions, liked videos and playlists, so they can pick something to watch. The app never posts, comments, likes, subscribes or changes anything on the account.
