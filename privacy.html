Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Screening Room is a personal, non-commercial YouTube player. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by that household. This policy explains what happens to Google account data when the optional YouTube sign-in is used.

What the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube account, the app asks Google for read-only access (youtube.readonly). It uses that access to show:

  • your channel name and profile picture, to show which account is connected;
  • your subscriptions, to list recent uploads from channels you follow;
  • your liked videos; and
  • your playlists and the videos in them.

The app cannot post, comment, like, subscribe or change anything on your account.

How it is used and stored

This data is used only to show these lists inside the app, so you can choose what to watch. The app stores one item: the refresh token Google issues at sign-in. It is kept in a file readable only by the app's own user account on the owner's computer. Lists fetched from YouTube are held in memory for up to a few hours and are not saved. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Removing access

Press Disconnect in the app. That deletes the stored token and revokes it with Google. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Google and YouTube

Screening Room uses YouTube API Services. By connecting an account, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Screening Room's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
